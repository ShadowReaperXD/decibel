# Decibel

Decibel is a lightweight audio utility for Minecraft that gives you granular, precise control over your in-game soundscape. Instead of relying on broad, all-or-nothing vanilla sound categories like "Blocks," "Players," or "Hostile Creatures," Decibel adds an intuitive tweak menu that allows you to adjust the volume of individual game sounds independently.
## Features:
 * **Per-Sound Volume Control:** Adjust or mute specific individual sound events (like nether portal hums, phantom screeches, or anvil drops) without affecting the rest of the category. Quick individual icon buttons let you instantly reset a single sound back to default.
 * **Sound Presets System:** Save your custom sound balances into named presets and switch between them on the fly. Easily create, apply, update, and manage custom audio profiles for PvP, building, farming, or quiet night sessions.
 * **Category & Mod Filtering:** Instantly filter registered sounds using dedicated tabs for Vanilla, Mods, or All to quickly find and manage audio cues added by other mod packs.
 * **In-Game Search & Tooltips:** Search through all sound events in real time using human-readable sound names, complete with hover tooltips displaying the exact raw resource locations (e.g., minecraft:ambient.cave).
 * **Seamless Fabric Integration:** Lightweight, purely client-side architecture with custom square icon texture buttons designed to blend naturally into vanilla Minecraft screens.
